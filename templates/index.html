How PPBS builds evidence

A layered evidence profile

From reconstruction to generalisation

PPBS does not force every claim into a single replicated-or-not label. It records what each study design can establish, where the evidence travels, and what remains uncertain.

Reconstruct

Recover the sample, items, scoring, recodes, exclusions, missingness, and weights behind a claim.

Reproduce

Check whether the documented data, code, and computing environment recover the reported result.

Stress-test

Examine defensible alternatives whose assumptions could materially change the conclusion.

Confirm independently

Test the claim in a separate cross-section or confirmatory sample rather than reusing the discovery sample.

Track over time

Distinguish recurrence across election cycles from within-person change in longitudinal recontacts.

Generalise carefully

Compare populations, periods, countries, providers, and surveys only after checking design and measurement.
