# PPBS Google Forms specification

These forms are intake and correction mechanisms. A submission does not automatically determine public credit, authorship, investigator status, or funding acknowledgement. PPBS verifies submissions with the relevant project team before updating public records.

## Form 1: PPBS contribution record correction or addition

**Live form:** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfQJUxmX5mlhyAs4b1qVBT7TDVL0tHj5PJpfZCtLYXd5msH_A/viewform

**Description**

Use this form if you contributed to PPBS and are missing from the public contribution register, or if a project or role is incomplete. PPBS will review the information with the relevant project team before updating the public record. Submission does not by itself determine authorship.

**Questions**

1. Full name — short answer, required
2. Email address — short answer, required; kept private
3. ORCID iD — short answer, optional
4. Current affiliation — short answer, optional
5. Project or output — short answer, required
6. What did you contribute? — paragraph, required
7. CRediT roles — checkboxes: Conceptualization; Data curation; Formal analysis; Funding acquisition; Investigation; Methodology; Project administration; Resources; Software; Supervision; Validation; Visualization; Writing – original draft; Writing – review & editing
8. PPBS-specific roles — checkboxes: Translation; Country adaptation; Political/context review; Back-translation; Mentoring; Research operations; Data governance; Country coordination; Community engagement; Funding support
9. Evidence, relevant link, approximate date, or team contact — paragraph, required
10. Preferred public name and affiliation — short answer, optional
11. Consent — checkbox, required: “I consent to PPBS contacting me and, after verification, displaying the approved name, affiliation, project, and roles on the public website.”
12. Notes or confidentiality considerations — paragraph, optional

## Form 2: PPBS investigator and participant profile addition or update

**Live form:** https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfXSIcKj6EX-PLEl429d7CnVfM5qcn01iNkmG6EXUAE_ggPNg/viewform

**Description**

Use this form to add or update your participation or profile on the PPBS investigators page. PPBS will verify current and historical involvement before publication.

**Questions**

1. Full name — short answer, required
2. Email address — short answer, required; kept private
3. Request type — multiple choice, required: Add a new profile; Update an existing profile; Correct or remove inaccurate information
4. Current or past PPBS participation — checkboxes, required: Researcher; Collaborator; Funder or resource supporter; Country team; Past researcher; Mentor; Other
5. PPBS projects, outputs, and approximate dates — paragraph, required
6. Role or participation description — paragraph, required
7. Current affiliation and title — short answer, optional
8. Public profile links — paragraph, optional: ORCID, institutional page, personal website, Google Scholar, or another relevant profile
9. Public photo URL — short answer, optional; only submit a photo you have permission to use
10. Short public bio — paragraph, optional; up to 150 words
11. Evidence or a PPBS team contact who can verify the information — paragraph, required
12. Consent — checkbox, required: “I consent to PPBS contacting me and, after verification, publishing the approved profile and participation information on the PPBS website.”
13. Notes or confidentiality considerations — paragraph, optional
