The Psychology of Political Behavior Studies (PPBS) is a cumulative political-psychology research programme and research infrastructure. Its documented U.S. core brings recurring measures of political attitudes, identities, values, vote choice, participation, and psychological dispositions into a connected set of studies.

Across the documented 2016, 2018, and 2020 election cycles, PPBS comprises 12 datasets and 21,056 interviews: four national quota cross-sections of U.S. adults (N=7,259), five independent confirmatory or replication samples (N=11,582), and three longitudinal recontact samples (N=2,215). These designs support different claims, so PPBS reports cross-sectional, independent-sample, and within-person evidence separately.
