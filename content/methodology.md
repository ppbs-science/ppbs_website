PPBS comprises 12 datasets collected across three U.S. election cycles: four national cross-sections, five independent confirmatory or replication samples, and three longitudinal recontact samples. The surveys were fielded by professional survey companies using quota-based sampling; the recontact waves followed earlier respondents to support within-person analyses over time.

## Surveys by Sample Type

### National Cross-sections (N=7,259)

1. 2016 Pre-election sample (N=1,500)
2. 2018 Pre-election sample (N=1,000)
3. 2018 Post-election sample (N=2,759)
4. 2020 Pre-election sample (N=2,000)

### Recontacts / Longitudinal Panels (N=2,215)
