PPBS comprises 12 datasets collected across three U.S. election cycles: four national cross-sections, five independent confirmatory or replication samples, and three longitudinal recontact samples. The surveys were fielded by professional survey companies using quota-based sampling; the recontact waves followed earlier respondents to support within-person analyses over time.

## A Layered Evidence Profile

PPBS evaluates a claim across six connected dimensions. Not every project will reach every layer, and the layers are not interchangeable. The result is a multidimensional evidence profile rather than a binary replicated-or-not label.

1. **Reconstructability:** Can another researcher recover the sample, items, response options, scoring, recodes, missing-data rules, exclusions, and weights used for the claim?
2. **Computational reproducibility:** Do the documented data, code, software, and computing environment recover the reported result?
3. **Analytical robustness:** Does the conclusion survive defensible alternative operationalisations and analyses tied to explicit assumptions?
4. **Independent-sample confirmation:** Does the pattern recur in a separate PPBS cross-section or confirmatory sample rather than the sample in which it was first identified?
5. **Evidence over time:** Does the pattern recur across election cycles? Where recontact data exist, do within-person analyses support a longitudinal claim? These are reported separately.
6. **Cross-context generalisation:** Does comparable evidence recur across populations, periods, countries, providers, or survey infrastructures after differences in design and measurement have been checked?

### The Claim-level Research Record

Each verified claim should have a versioned record containing the estimand; exact items and response options; sample and variable provenance; scoring, recoding, missingness, exclusion, and weighting decisions; validated analysis code and computing environment; robustness checks; comparison samples; review decisions; known limitations; and unresolved questions. This record makes corrections and extensions traceable without pretending that every uncertainty has been resolved.

PPBS is also developing an item-level question bank. Mappings between items and constructs will be labelled as **documented**, **expert-validated**, **empirically supported**, **provisional**, **rejected**, or **unresolved**. Similar construct names alone are not enough to establish measurement equivalence.
