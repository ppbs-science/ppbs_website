PPBS treats research, infrastructure, mentoring, governance, dissemination, translation, and country adaptation as contributions that should be made visible. The public register shows who contributed, to which project, and in what role, using CRediT roles alongside PPBS-specific roles.

> **Evidence-review preview:** the records below use current project files, repository history, and team correspondence. They are being shown locally for contributor confirmation before public release. Evidence status is displayed for each project; no private funding amounts are published.

Country-team work is broader than literal translation. It can include conceptual review, local political and cultural adaptation, back-translation, validation, project administration, and interpretation. A public record appears only after the contributor and project team have approved the fields shown here.

This page is generated from project-level Tenzing records. Email addresses, private funding amounts, conflicts of interest, and other governance information remain outside the public website.

**Is a contribution missing, incomplete, or inaccurate?** Use the [PPBS contribution record correction or addition form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfQJUxmX5mlhyAs4b1qVBT7TDVL0tHj5PJpfZCtLYXd5msH_A/viewform). Every submission sends an email notification to the PPBS PI. PPBS will review the information with the relevant project team and correct the public register where appropriate.
